## 2024-05-19 - React.memo() with Complex Message List Components
**Learning:** In React streaming chat interfaces, applying `React.memo()` to complex child components within message lists (like `SupervisorRail`, `TraceAccordion`, `PlanCard`, `ArtifactBadgeRow`, and `PlanConfirmationCard`) is crucial for optimal performance, especially during rapid streaming token updates. But passing inline functions such as inline `handleExecuteToolCalls` or `handleCopy` as props defeats `React.memo()`. You cannot use `React.useCallback` inline within JSX (e.g., `onClick={React.useCallback(...)}`), as this violates React's Rules of Hooks. Hooks must be declared at the top level of the component body.
**Action:** Always wrap list child components with `React.memo()` and define memoized callbacks (`useCallback`) at the top level of the parent component body to prevent referential inequality and subsequent expensive re-renders.
